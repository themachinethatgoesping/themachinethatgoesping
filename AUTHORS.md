# Authors and copyright

`themachinethatgoesping` is a community effort. Copyright is held collectively by
the **themachinethatgoesping contributors** listed below.

Every source file therefore carries a notice of the form:

```text
SPDX-FileCopyrightText: <year(s)> themachinethatgoesping contributors (see AUTHORS.md)
SPDX-License-Identifier: MPL-2.0
```

The project keeps the same license as before: most files are covered by the
Mozilla Public License 2.0 (`MPL-2.0`, see [`LICENSE`](LICENSE)). Some auxiliary
files (build, CI and configuration files) are instead released under `CC0-1.0`,
and a few third-party pieces under `MIT`. The authoritative license for any
given file is the one named in its own SPDX header; full license texts live in
the [`LICENSES/`](LICENSES) directory.

## Why a collective copyright line?

Over the lifetime of the project, contributions have been made by the same
people under different institutional affiliations (for example, the same author
while employed by different institutions at different times). Rather than
tracking every institution in the header of every file, we follow the common
open-source practice used by projects such as the Linux kernel, Godot and GNOME:
the source files credit the *contributors* collectively, and this file records
who those contributors are, together with the affiliation(s) under which they
contributed.

Affiliations reflect the institution under which the work was carried out **at
the time of the contribution**. They do not imply any current relationship with,
or endorsement by, those institutions. Copyright in each contribution remains
with its author and/or that author's affiliated institution, as applicable.
